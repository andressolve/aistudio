The Hagiographer's Rule

Saints aren't statues, and this isn't Wikipedia. Don't try to cover the whole life — but don't just stack disconnected moments either. Pick two or three moments that trace how he became who he was: a before, a turn, an after. For example: the merchant's son before, the turning point, the man he became after — that's an arc, not a slideshow. The icon is the simplification; your job is to show the becoming.
